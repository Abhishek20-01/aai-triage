🎯 Problem Statement

The Core Challenge

Healthcare providers have access to fragmented patient information spread across multiple visits and facilities. The goal is to design an AI-native clinical assistant that helps doctors instantly understand a patient's relevant medical history.

🩺
Doctor Pain Points
PHC doctors start every consultation from scratch, manually gathering patient history, understanding previous treatments, and identifying risk factors.
  • Must manually gather patient history every visit
  • Records spread across multiple facilities
  • No alerts for allergies, chronic conditions
  • Complex medical histories hard to recall
  • Time spent on context instead of diagnosis
👤
Patient Pain Points
Patients must recount their medical history at every visit, leading to inconsistent care, delayed diagnoses, and poor follow-up.
  • Must repeat medical history at every visit
  • Inconsistent care from different doctors
  • Critical conditions missed due to lack of context
  • Missed appointments and medication non-adherence
  • No access to records across facilities
🔬 Key Research Focus

Five Critical Questions

Our research addresses the fundamental challenges in PHC clinical workflows and digital health record utilization.

📋
How do PHC doctors currently access patient history?
Understanding the current workflow, tools used, time spent, and pain points in accessing patient records during consultations.
🎯
What information is most critical for clinical decisions?
Identifying which data points (allergies, medications, lab trends, chronic conditions) matter most for faster, informed decisions.
🤖
How can AI summarize longitudinal records?
Designing AI systems that condense years of medical history into actionable insights without losing critical context.
🔒
What consent and privacy mechanisms are required?
Building robust ABHA consent management that ensures patient privacy while enabling seamless clinical workflows.
⚠️
How to handle incomplete or missing records?
Designing graceful degradation and fallback mechanisms when health records are incomplete, inconsistent, or unavailable.

🏗️ Technical Architecture

System Design & Integration

A comprehensive architecture designed for scalability, security, and seamless ABHA integration.

System Overview

AarogyaAI is an AI-native diagnostic triage assistant that integrates with the Ayushman Bharat Digital Health Mission (ABDM) ecosystem to provide PHC doctors with instant, relevant patient context during consultations.

Architecture Components

  • Frontend: Next.js 14 with TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Recharts for data visualization
  • Backend: Node.js with Next.js API Routes, PostgreSQL database
  • AI/ML Engine: OpenAI GPT-4 / Claude for medical summarization, custom ML models for risk scoring
  • Infrastructure: Vercel (frontend), Railway/Render (backend), Redis caching

ABHA Integration Flow

Authentication → Consent → Data Retrieval → AI Processing → Presentation

The system authenticates doctors via ABHA OAuth 2.0, requests patient consent through ABDM, retrieves health records from ABHA Health Locker, processes them through the AI engine, and presents prioritized insights to the doctor.

Data Models

Complete TypeScript interfaces for patients, visits, vitals, prescriptions, lab results, and AI insights.

Entity Key Fields Purpose
Patient abhaId, demographics, bloodGroup, allergies, emergencyContact Patient identification and safety
Visit date, type, diagnosis, vitals, prescriptions, labResults Clinical encounter tracking
AIInsight summary, criticalAlerts, riskFactors, recommendations, urgencyScore AI-generated clinical intelligence
Consent patientId, permissions, grantedAt, expiresAt, accessLog ABDM compliance and audit

Security & Compliance

  • ABHA OAuth 2.0 authentication with JWT tokens
  • End-to-end encryption (AES-256) for data at rest and in transit
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) for doctors, nurses, and admins
  • Comprehensive audit logging for all data access
  • DPDP Act compliance with granular, revocable consent
  • TLS 1.3 for all API communications
